## Attribution and source behavior

- Verified sources require title, HTTPS URL, publisher, snippet, retrieval time, provider provenance, and content hash.
- Exact-frequency wording is qualified as an inferred recurring-process interpretation unless evidence establishes a statistical ranking.
- Every creative candidate carries sourced/inferred/speculative labels, confidence, ambiguity, alternatives, model recommendations, holdouts, and an attribution correction mechanism.
- Prompt-injection text remains marked as untrusted source data.
- Research-provider absence blocks factual attribution with zero mutation or permits only an explicitly speculative, citation-free exercise.
